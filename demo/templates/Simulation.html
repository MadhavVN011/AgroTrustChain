WTH-Raft Simulation

A simulation of the paper's Weighted Two-Hop Raft consensus against standard Raft and PBFT. It uses a message-level model for latency and throughput, and a round-based model with malicious nodes that uses the same reputation rules as the TraceabilityManagement contract. The full tables, the check of each claim in the paper, and the model's assumptions are in RESULTS.md.

Key assumption: after every round, an off-chain inspection reveals the truth, and every node's report is scored against it. With a weaker oracle, malicious nodes would keep their weight for longer.
